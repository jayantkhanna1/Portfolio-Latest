About

Hi, I'm Jayant Khanna, a Software Engineer from India. I build web applications, mobile apps, backend systems, and occasionally things nobody asked for simply because I wanted to see if they could be built.

I'm currently a Founding Engineer at Roomrr, where I lead backend engineering and work alongside the founders to build the product from the ground up. Startup life comes with multiple hats, constantly evolving challenges, and plenty of opportunities to learn, which is exactly what I enjoy most about it.

Before Roomrr, I spent nearly three years as a Data Engineer, working with Databricks, PySpark, NLP, anomaly detection, and large-scale data pipelines. One of the biggest lessons from that experience? There's a considerable difference between code that works and code that works on 40 billion rows.

I've also been freelancing since 2021, delivering 29 live websites for 13 clients across fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, and several other industries. My technical expertise spans Python, Django, React, React Native, and Generative AI, including RAG systems, LLM integrations, and intelligent automation.

Beyond my professional work, I'm naturally curious about technology. I enjoy experimenting with new ideas, solving unconventional problems, and finding simpler ways to build complicated systems. Sometimes, the best projects are the ones that start without a clear roadmap.

If you're looking for someone to build your next product, automate something painfully repetitive, or turn an ambitious idea into working software, I'd love to hear from you.

I'm particularly fond of conversations that begin with "I'm not sure if this is possible, but..."
